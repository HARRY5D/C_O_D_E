# Home Loan Tax Benefits
# Source: Sections 24(b), 80C, 80EEA | Income Tax Act, 1961 | FY 2025-26

## Overview

A home loan provides tax benefits under **two separate sections** — one for interest payment and one for principal repayment. An additional deduction exists for first-time homebuyers under Section 80EEA.

---

## Section 24(b) — Interest on Home Loan

| Property Type | Deduction Limit |
|---|---|
| Self-Occupied Property | **₹2,00,000** per year |
| Let-Out / Deemed Let-Out | **Unlimited** (full interest deductible) |

### Conditions
- Loan must be for **construction or purchase** (not repair — that's limited to ₹30,000)
- Construction must be completed within **5 years** from year of loan
- If construction is not completed in time: limit reduces to ₹30,000

### Pre-Construction Interest
- Interest paid before the year of possession can be claimed in **5 equal installments** starting from the year of possession

---

## Section 80C — Principal Repayment

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum deduction | Part of overall ₹1,50,000 80C limit |
| Property type | Self-occupied only |

**Note**: This is part of the ₹1,50,000 80C limit (shared with ELSS, PPF, etc.)

---

## Section 80EEA — Additional Interest Deduction (First-Time Buyers)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Additional limit | ₹1,50,000 per year |
| Stamp duty value | Property stamp duty value ≤ ₹45 Lakhs |
| Loan sanction period | April 1, 2019 – March 31, 2022 |
| Condition | Should not own any other property |

---

## Total Possible Deduction

| Section | Max Benefit |
|---|---|
| Section 24(b) — Interest | ₹2,00,000 |
| Section 80EEA — Additional Interest | ₹1,50,000 |
| Section 80C — Principal | ₹1,50,000 (within overall 80C cap) |
| **Total** | **₹5,00,000** (for eligible first-time buyers) |

---

## Joint Home Loan

If a home loan is taken jointly:
- Each co-borrower can claim **their share** of interest under Section 24(b)
- Each can claim up to ₹2,00,000 individually
- Both must be co-owners of the property

---

## Regime Comparison

| Deduction | Old Regime | New Regime |
|---|---|---|
| Section 24(b) — Interest | ✅ Up to ₹2,00,000 | ❌ Not allowed (self-occupied) |
| Section 80C — Principal | ✅ Within ₹1.5L | ❌ Not allowed |
| Section 80EEA | ✅ ₹1,50,000 | ❌ Not allowed |

**Note**: Let-out property interest can be claimed in New Regime against rental income.
