# Section 80C — Deductions under Chapter VI-A
# Source: Income Tax Act, 1961 | FY 2025-26 | AY 2026-27

## Overview

Section 80C of the Income Tax Act allows individuals (and HUFs) to claim deductions of up to **₹1,50,000** per financial year from their gross total income. This is available **only under the Old Tax Regime**. Under the New Tax Regime, Section 80C deductions are **not applicable**.

## Limit

- **Maximum deduction**: ₹1,50,000 per year
- If total of all 80C investments exceeds ₹1,50,000, only ₹1,50,000 is allowed as deduction
- Additional NPS investment beyond 80C → covered under **Section 80CCD(1B)**

---

## Key Rules

1. Only individuals and HUFs can claim 80C deductions
2. Deduction is from **Gross Total Income** (not salary alone)
3. **Old Regime only** — New Regime taxpayers cannot claim 80C
4. ELSS has **3-year lock-in** — shortest among 80C instruments
5. PPF withdrawals are **completely tax-free** (EEE status)
6. EPF interest is tax-free up to ₹2.5 lakh per year (employee contribution limit)
