# Section 80D — Health Insurance Deductions
# Source: Income Tax Act, 1961 | FY 2025-26 | AY 2026-27

## Overview

Section 80D allows deduction for **health insurance premiums** paid for self, family, and parents. Available under **Old Regime only**. Not available under New Tax Regime.

---

## Deduction Limits

| Category | Age < 60 Years | Age 60+ (Senior Citizen) |
|---|---|---|
| Self + Spouse + Children | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 |
| Parents | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 |
| **Maximum Total** | **₹50,000** | **₹1,00,000** |

---

## Eligible Payments

1. **Health Insurance Premium** — Any recognized insurer (e.g., Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Max Bupa)
2. **Preventive Health Check-up** — Up to ₹5,000 (within the overall limit)
3. **Medical Expenditure for Senior Citizens** — If no health insurance policy exists (₹50,000 limit)

---

## Key Rules

1. Payment must be made **other than cash** (except preventive checkup)
2. Premium paid for **siblings is not eligible** (only self, spouse, children, parents)
3. Premium must be paid **during the financial year**
4. If employer pays on group mediclaim, individual cannot claim that premium

---

## Examples

**Example 1: Age 35, no parents covered**
- Premium paid: ₹22,000
- Deduction: ₹22,000 (within ₹25,000 limit)

**Example 2: Age 35 + Senior Citizen Parents (60+)**
- Self premium: ₹20,000
- Parent premium: ₹45,000
- Deduction: ₹20,000 + ₹45,000 = **₹65,000**

**Example 3: Senior Citizen (age 62)**
- Premium: ₹60,000
- Deduction: ₹50,000 (capped at ₹50,000)

---

## Preventive Health Check-up

- Max ₹5,000 within the applicable limit
- Cash payment allowed
- Covers self, spouse, children, parents
