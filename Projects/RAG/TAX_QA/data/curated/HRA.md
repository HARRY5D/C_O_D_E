# HRA — House Rent Allowance Exemption
# Source: Section 10(13A) | Income Tax Act, 1961 | FY 2025-26

## Overview

HRA (House Rent Allowance) received from employer is partially or fully exempt from tax under **Section 10(13A)** in the **Old Tax Regime**. Not available under New Regime.

---

## Exemption Formula

HRA exemption = **Minimum of** the following three:

| Component | Formula |
|---|---|
| Actual HRA received | As per salary slip |
| City-based percentage | 50% of Basic Salary (Metro) / 40% (Non-Metro) |
| Rent minus 10% | Rent Paid − 10% of Basic Salary |

---

## Metro vs Non-Metro Cities

| Metro (50%) | Non-Metro (40%) |
|---|---|
| Mumbai | All other cities |
| Delhi | |
| Chennai | |
| Kolkata | |

---

## Key Rules

1. **Must pay rent** — HRA exemption requires actual rent payment
2. **PAN of landlord required** if rent > ₹1,00,000/year (₹8,333/month)
3. HRA is **not available if you live in your own house**
4. If both HRA and home loan interest exist simultaneously:
   - HRA exemption allowed if the rented house and owned house are in **different cities**
5. Self-employed individuals cannot claim HRA exemption (they use Section 80GG instead)

---

## Section 80GG — For Self-Employed / No HRA Salary

If you don't receive HRA, claim under **Section 80GG**:
- Deduction = Minimum of:
  - Rent paid − 10% of adjusted gross income
  - 25% of adjusted gross income
  - ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000 per year)

---

## Example Calculation

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Basic Salary (monthly) | ₹50,000 |
| HRA received (monthly) | ₹20,000 |
| Rent paid (monthly) | ₹18,000 |
| City | Delhi (Metro) |

**Annual calculation:**
- Component 1: ₹20,000 × 12 = ₹2,40,000
- Component 2: 50% × ₹50,000 × 12 = ₹3,00,000
- Component 3: (₹18,000 − 10% × ₹50,000) × 12 = ₹1,56,000

**HRA Exemption = Min(₹2,40,000, ₹3,00,000, ₹1,56,000) = ₹1,56,000**
