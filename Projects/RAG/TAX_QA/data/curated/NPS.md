# National Pension System (NPS) — Tax Deductions
# Source: Income Tax Act, 1961 | FY 2025-26 | AY 2026-27

## Overview

NPS contributions qualify for deductions under **three separate sections**, providing the highest tax efficiency among all instruments when fully utilized.

---

## Deduction Sections

### Section 80CCD(1) — Employee's Own Contribution

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Eligible amount | Up to 10% of Basic Salary |
| Overall cap | Within ₹1,50,000 (80C limit) |
| Regime | Old Regime only |

### Section 80CCD(1B) — Additional Employee Contribution

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Eligible amount | Up to ₹50,000 |
| Nature | **Over and above** the ₹1,50,000 80C limit |
| Total max benefit | ₹1,50,000 (80C) + ₹50,000 (80CCD1B) = **₹2,00,000** |
| Regime | Old Regime only |

### Section 80CCD(2) — Employer's Contribution

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Private sector | Up to 10% of Basic Salary |
| Government employees | Up to 14% of Basic Salary |
| Nature | Separate from 80C; deducted from gross salary |
| Regime | **Available in BOTH Old and New Regime** |

---

## Maximum Tax Benefit (Combined)

| Section | Limit |
|---|---|
| 80C (includes 80CCD1) | ₹1,50,000 |
| 80CCD(1B) | ₹50,000 |
| 80CCD(2) | 10%/14% of Basic |
| **Grand Total** | **₹2,00,000 + Employer NPS** |

---

## NPS Account Types

- **Tier-I**: Mandatory; tax deduction available; restricted withdrawal
- **Tier-II**: Voluntary; no tax deduction; free withdrawal

---

## Key Rules

1. Must have active Tier-I NPS account (PRAN)
2. Minimum contribution: ₹500/month or ₹6,000/year
3. 80CCD(2) is the **only NPS deduction available under New Regime**
4. On maturity, 60% lump sum is tax-free; 40% must be annuitized
