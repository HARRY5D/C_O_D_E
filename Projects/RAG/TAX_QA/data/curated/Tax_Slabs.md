## Surcharge

| Total Income | Surcharge Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs.50 Lakh | Nil |
| Rs.50 Lakh - Rs.1 Crore | 10% |
| Rs.1 Crore - Rs.2 Crore | 15% |
| Rs.2 Crore - Rs.5 Crore | 25% |

**4%** on (Income Tax + Surcharge). Applicable to all taxpayers, both regimes.

## Rebate under Section 87A

| Regime | Income Limit | Max Rebate | Net Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old Regime | Rs.5,00,000 | Rs.12,500 | Zero tax on income up to Rs.5L |
| New Regime | Rs.12,00,000 | Rs.60,000 | Zero tax on income up to Rs.12L |

**CRITICAL RULE (Budget 2025):** Under New Tax Regime for FY 2025-26, any individual with
Net Taxable Income up to Rs.12,00,000 pays ZERO income tax due to Section 87A rebate.
This applies AFTER slab tax is computed — the rebate completely wipes out the tax.

Example: Net taxable income = Rs.7,50,000
  Slab tax = 5% of Rs.3,50,000 = Rs.17,500
  Section 87A rebate = Rs.17,500 (full, since income below Rs.12L limit)
  Final tax = Rs.0

## Quick Tax Estimates — FREELANCERS under Section 44ADA (New Regime, after Rebate)

For freelancers and professionals: Taxable income = 50% of Gross Receipts.
No standard deduction. No Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80D, NPS) under New Regime.
Section 87A Rebate applies: if presumptive profit (50% of gross) is up to Rs.12 Lakh, tax is ZERO.

| Gross Receipts | Presumptive Profit (50%) | New Regime Tax | Old Regime Tax (no deductions) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rs.10,00,000 | Rs.5,00,000 | Rs.0 (87A rebate) | Rs.52,000 |
| Rs.15,00,000 | Rs.7,50,000 | Rs.0 (87A rebate) | Rs.65,000 |
| Rs.20,00,000 | Rs.10,00,000 | Rs.0 (87A rebate) | Rs.1,08,160 |
| Rs.24,00,000 | Rs.12,00,000 | Rs.0 (87A rebate, at limit) | Rs.1,72,380 |
| Rs.30,00,000 | Rs.15,00,000 | ~Rs.1,17,000 | ~Rs.2,34,000 |
| Rs.40,00,000 | Rs.20,00,000 | ~Rs.2,08,000 | ~Rs.4,29,000 |
| Rs.75,00,000 | Rs.37,50,000 | ~Rs.9,12,000 | ~Rs.11,96,000 |

**Key insight:** For gross receipts up to Rs.24 Lakh, a freelancer under Section 44ADA
pays ZERO tax under the New Regime due to the Section 87A rebate.
