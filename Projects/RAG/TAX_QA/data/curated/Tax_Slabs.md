# Tax Slabs — FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27
# Source: Finance Act 2025, Budget 2025 | Income Tax Act, 1961

## Old Tax Regime — Slabs

| Income Range | Tax Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | 0% | Basic exemption limit |
| ₹2,50,001 – ₹5,00,000 | 5% | Rebate u/s 87A may nullify this |
| ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 20% | |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% | |

**Basic Exemption Limit:**
- Individuals below 60 years: ₹2,50,000
- Senior Citizens (60–80 years): ₹3,00,000
- Super Senior Citizens (80+): ₹5,00,000

## Surcharge

| Total Income | Surcharge Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹50 Lakh | Nil |
| ₹50 Lakh – ₹1 Crore | 10% |
| ₹1 Crore – ₹2 Crore | 15% |
| ₹2 Crore – ₹5 Crore | 25% |
| Above ₹5 Crore | 25% (capped at 25% for New Regime) |

**Note**: Marginal relief applies to prevent excess burden at slab boundaries.

---

## Health & Education Cess

**4%** on (Income Tax + Surcharge)

Applicable to all taxpayers, both regimes.
